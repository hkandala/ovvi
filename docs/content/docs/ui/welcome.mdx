---
title: "Welcome"
description: "The welcome panel: what Ovvi is, on the first launch"
---

A floating panel over Ovvi's windows, 960 × 640 pt (scaled down as a whole to 80% of a smaller screen), centered: no title bar and no window buttons, rounded
corners, the window's glass (Classic: opaque), a shadow. Drag its background to move it. It shows a few pages, one
idea each, and the user turns them with Next and Back, → and ←, or Return. Skip (or Esc) closes it from any page;
Get Started closes it on the last.

- **When.** On the first launch, alone: no window opens until it closes, and then the first one does (the
  [new-window view](/docs/ui/window#new-window)). Windows macOS saved before (a reset leaves them, as their folder
  needs Full Disk Access) close at launch and connect to nothing, so macOS forgets them. Get Started,
  Skip or Esc mark it done (`welcomeDone`),
  and it never opens by itself again; a quit with it open does not, so the next launch shows it again. Settings ▸
  General ▸ Welcome to Ovvi ▸ Show Welcome opens it again. It hides while Ovvi is not the active app, and the menu
  bar's keys do nothing behind it but Quit, Hide, Settings and Copy. `scripts/e2e.sh` marks it done for the UI
  tests, and a test that needs it passes `-welcomeDone NO`. `defaults delete dev.ovvi.app.debug welcomeDone` shows a
  first launch again in a Debug build.
- **herdr.** When This Mac has no herdr, one page installs it: one line on what it is, Install (the same install
  as the not-installed page, [states](/docs/ui/states)), its progress, and a failure with Retry. Next waits until
  herdr is found; Skip still closes. With herdr installed the page is not there.
- **Nothing changes in the app** while it shows: its pictures are pictures.
- **Reduce Motion:** pages cross-fade, nothing moves.

## Living pictures

- **The intro.** The logo's three panes come in back to front with the name under them. Then they slide apart and
  resize into the window in the first page's picture, its sidebar and two panes, as the name fades out, and the
  picture fades in over them. A key or a click goes straight to the first page. About 2 s.
- **Five pages,** each a short silent clip of the app over the whole panel: sessions keep running (a dev server's
  log and a build beside it); panes, tabs and spaces (a split, a tab, then a space no other page shows); an agent's
  finished turn in its terminal and in Chat, two pictures faded into each other; Claude Code opening Git and a plugin beside it and a browser in a new tab; Add
  Machine with an SSH target typed in. The title, its line and the buttons sit at the panel's foot, over a shade
  180 pt tall (black in dark, white in light): fully dark at the panel's edge, fading out above the title. The words
  carry a soft glow of the same color.
- **The clips** show the app's whole window, its title row and buttons, as it is: no zoom, 3:2 like the panel, in
  the app's appearance (dark or light, switched while the panel shows), over a plain black or white desktop. A page's
  clip starts from its beginning when the page shows and loops while it stays; pages turn only on Next, Back or the
  keys. Pages cross-fade, the new clip from a touch larger. Reduce Motion: each clip's frame where its change shows,
  still.
- **The herdr step** (last, only without herdr): herdr's own logo, as it is, on a small tile with a hairline border,
  Install herdr and its command, its progress, then a green check on the same tile, "herdr is installed" and the
  title "You're all set". The line under the title says why herdr.
- `scripts/record-onboarding.sh` records the clips and the welcome's films again, in one go, on a quiet Mac.
