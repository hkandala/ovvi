---
title: "Welcome"
description: "The welcome panel: an annotated tour of one Ovvi window, on the first launch"
---

A floating panel over Ovvi's windows, 960 × 640 pt (scaled down as a whole to 80% of a smaller screen), centred
on the screen: no title bar and no window buttons, rounded corners,
the window's glass (Classic: opaque), a shadow. Drag its background to move it. A picture of a real Ovvi window
fills its top; a band below holds the page's title, one sentence, Skip, the page dots, Back and Next. The user
turns the pages with Next and Back, → and ←, or Return. Skip (or Esc) closes it from any page; Get Started closes
it on the last.

- **The intro.** The app icon fades and scales in, a soft light sweeps once across it, and the name "Ovvi" fades
  in under it (about 2 s). Then it dissolves into the first page. A click, Return, → or Space goes to the first
  page at once; Esc skips the welcome.
- **The pages.** One window, in six pages: what Ovvi is (the whole window, labelled: spaces, tabs, panes, an
  agent), panes and splits, tabs and spaces, agents and the chat view, plugins, and remote machines. On each page
  the picture pans and zooms to the part the words are about, then callouts draw on: a dot on the thing, a thin
  line, and a label with its keys (⌘D, ⇧⌘D, ⌘T, ⌃⌘T…).
- **The pictures.** 2x captures of Ovvi in a throwaway session (`scripts/welcome-pictures.sh`), each in light and
  dark: the panel shows the set of the app's appearance, and switches when it changes.
- **When.** On the first launch, over the new-window view. Get Started, Skip or Esc mark it done (`welcomeDone`),
  and it never opens by itself again; a quit with it open does not, so the next launch shows it again. Settings ▸
  General ▸ Welcome to Ovvi ▸ Show Welcome opens it again. It hides while Ovvi is not the active app, and the menu
  bar's keys do nothing behind it but Quit, Hide and Settings. A run by hand of a test or Debug build passes
  `-welcomeDone YES`; `defaults delete dev.ovvi.app.debug welcomeDone` shows a first launch again.
- **herdr.** When This Mac has no herdr, one more page comes last: one line on what it is, Install (the same
  install as the not-installed page, [states](/docs/ui/states)), its progress, and a failure with Retry. Get
  Started waits until herdr is found; Skip still closes. With herdr installed the page is not there.
- **Nothing changes in the app** while it shows: its pictures are pictures.
- **Reduce Motion:** pages cross-fade with no pan or zoom, the callouts show at once, and the intro is a plain fade.
