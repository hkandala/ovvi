---
title: "Welcome"
description: "The welcome panel: what Ovvi is, on the first launch"
---

A floating panel over Ovvi's windows, 960 × 640 pt (scaled down as a whole to 80% of a smaller screen), centred: no title bar and no window buttons, rounded
corners, the window's glass (Classic: opaque), a shadow. Drag its background to move it. It shows a few pages, one
idea each, and the user turns them with Next and Back, → and ←, or Return. Skip (or Esc) closes it from any page;
Get Started closes it on the last.

- **The intro** (about 2.5 s). The app's icon settles from 92 % and a soft blur to sharp, and the name "Ovvi" rises
  under it as its letters close up. Then the icon glides up into the panel's top left corner, where it stays as a
  small mark, and the first page fades in. A key or a click goes straight to the first page; Esc skips.
- **Pages.** Seven, one topic each: Welcome to Ovvi (sessions keep running), Panes and splits, Tabs, Spaces, Agents
  as a terminal or a chat, Plugins built in and your own, Any machine you can ssh to. Each has a picture on top, its
  title and two or three sentences below (keys as keycaps), and dots. The picture is a 2x capture of Ovvi's window,
  framed on the part the words talk about with a slow push-in (never past the capture's own size, so it stays sharp),
  one ring and a small label. The panes and plugins pages step through three pictures by themselves. Pages
  cross-fade with a slight slide.
- **Pictures** are HEIC files in the app, a dark and a light set; the panel shows the set of the app's appearance
  and switches with it. `scripts/welcome-scene.sh` makes them again.

- **When.** On the first launch, over the new-window view. Get Started, Skip or Esc mark it done (`welcomeDone`),
  and it never opens by itself again; a quit with it open does not, so the next launch shows it again. Settings ▸
  General ▸ Welcome to Ovvi ▸ Show Welcome opens it again. It hides while Ovvi is not the active app, and the menu
  bar's keys do nothing behind it but Quit, Hide and Settings. A run by hand of a test or Debug build passes
  `-welcomeDone YES`; `defaults delete dev.ovvi.app.debug welcomeDone` shows a first launch again.
- **herdr.** When This Mac has no herdr, one last page ("One more thing") installs it: one line on what it is,
  Install (the same install as the not-installed page, [states](/docs/ui/states)), its progress, and a failure with
  Retry. Get Started waits until herdr is found; Skip still closes. With herdr installed the page is not there.
- **Nothing changes in the app** while it shows: its pictures are pictures.
- **Reduce Motion:** the intro and the pages fade; a picture shows its last frame, still, with no steps.
