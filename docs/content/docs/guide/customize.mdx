---
title: "Customize"
description: "Change the look of Ovvi, its text size, the terminal font and cursor, motion and keyboard shortcuts."
---

Choose Ovvi ▸ Settings… (⌘,) to open the Settings window. A change takes effect at once in every window. The menu bar
and the command palette (⌘K) also have the most used switches. Every setting is listed in
[Settings](/docs/reference/settings).

## Light and dark

Ovvi has a light and a dark appearance. Dark is the default. To change it, do one of these:

- In Settings ▸ Appearance, click Auto, Light or Dark.
- Choose View ▸ Appearance, then Auto, Light or Dark.
- In the command palette, choose Appearance.

Auto follows the appearance of macOS.

## Liquid Glass

Liquid Glass is on by default. The desktop shows softly through the window, and the selected tab and space have a
glass look. With Liquid Glass off, the window is solid.

To turn it on or off, use the Liquid Glass switch in Settings ▸ Appearance, or choose View ▸ Liquid Glass.

In both looks, the panes are cards that float on the window, and the sidebar sits flat on it. The panels that float
over the window, such as the command palette, always use glass.

## Text size

The text size is the size of the text in every terminal. The default is 13 pt.

- Press ⌘+ to make the text larger, ⌘- to make it smaller, and ⌘0 to go back to the default.
- In Settings ▸ Appearance, use the Text size stepper. It goes from 8 to 32 pt.
- In the command palette, choose Font Size and pick a size.

When a browser card has the keyboard, the same keys zoom the web page instead.

## Terminal font and cursor

Settings ▸ Terminal changes every terminal at once:

- Font. JetBrains Mono comes with Ovvi. SF Mono and the other monospaced fonts on your Mac are in the same list.
- Cursor. Block, Bar or Underline. A program that asks for its own cursor shape gets it, for example the bar in vim's
  insert mode.
- Blink the cursor.
- Option key. With Types special characters, ⌥B types ∫. With Acts as Meta, ⌥ works as the Meta key that shells and
  editors use. Left acts as Meta does this only for the left Option key.
- Copy text when you select it. A selection goes to the clipboard as you make it.

The terminal colors follow the appearance: the Alabaster theme in light and the Afterglow theme in dark. There are no
other color themes.

## Animations

The Animations switch in Settings ▸ Appearance turns off all motion in Ovvi. With it off, every change shows at once:
no slides, fades or zooms. This includes the plugin cards.

## Accessibility

Ovvi follows the accessibility settings of macOS:

- Reduce Motion turns off the larger motions, such as slides and bounces. Short fades stay.
- Reduce Transparency makes the blur and the glass solid.
- Increase Contrast makes the dim text and lines of Ovvi stronger.

Other help for accessibility:

- A state never shows as a color alone. Each state icon has a word, and VoiceOver reads it, for example "codex, needs
  you" on a tab.
- VoiceOver can resize a split. Each divider is an adjustable element with its ratio as a percent.
- You can do every action from the keyboard, the menu bar or the command palette. ⌥⌘ and an arrow key move the
  keyboard between pane cards. Keyboard focus in the window follows Full Keyboard Access.

## Program icons

Each tab shows an icon for each program that runs in its panes. Ovvi draws these icons from icon sets with open
licenses.

## Change a shortcut

You can change the shortcut of every action, and add a second one. Open Settings ▸ Shortcuts, click a shortcut, and press the new keys. To see the most used shortcuts, press ⌘/. All the steps are in
[Change a shortcut](/docs/reference/shortcuts#change-a-shortcut).
